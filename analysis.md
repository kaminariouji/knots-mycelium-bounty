# Knots: a memecoin that spells its own thesis backwards

Most memecoins ask you to believe a story. Knots ($KNOTS) asks you to read one backwards. The ticker is $STONK reversed — same letters, other side of the wall — and the project leans into that inversion as its identity: a mark that flips, a landing page that turns over, a game where you jump between the two sides.

Under the flip there is an actual mechanism worth examining.

## What Knots is

Knots is a Solana token launched through the StonkFun launchpad. Total supply is 989,242,082 KNOTS (verified with `getTokenSupply` at slot 459,558,978). It trades on Raydium against STONK, the token it mirrors, and it is one of the projects inside the Mycelium Strategy's allocation set.

The pitch is not "number go up". It is a holder-reward loop: a **3% transfer tax on every $KNOTS transfer, on any venue**, collected and later paid out **in $STONK, pro-rata**, to wallets holding at least $20 of $KNOTS at the moment of distribution. Roughly 2.5% is deducted to cover the network costs of distributing. The tax is the product.

## What is live, and what is not

Live: trading on Jupiter and StonkFun, a reverse-jump game with a 50,000 STONK prize pool, and the tax accrual itself — every transfer feeds it.

Not live: the payouts. As of 21 October 2026 the project's own holder-rewards panel shows the distribution amount as "waiting to distribute" and payouts as "n/a". No holder has been paid from the tax yet. That is not a scandal — the site says the tax collects until it is worth distributing — but it is the difference between a designed loop and a demonstrated one.

## The numbers, read from the chain

From DexScreener on 21 October 2026: price $0.003665, fully diluted valuation about $3.63M, liquidity $339,769 in the KNOTS/STONK Raydium pool, and 24-hour volume of $85,523 across 127 buys and 183 sells. STONK trades near $0.1256 — which is what makes the reward denominated in something other than the token you already hold.

Two readings stand out. Volume is real relative to liquidity: a 25% volume-to-liquidity ratio in a day is not a dead pool. And sells outnumber buys 183 to 127 while price rose 31.5% over the same window — larger sells, smaller buys. That is a distribution pattern worth watching, not a verdict.

## One promising thing, one open question

**Promising:** the reward is denominated in a *different* asset. Paying holders in STONK means the payout is not self-referential — it does not need new KNOTS buyers to fund it, only transfer volume. If distributions begin and hold, that is a cash-flow story rather than a narrative one.

**The open question:** the loop has never completed a cycle. Until the first distribution lands, the 3% tax is a cost with no demonstrated return, and a round trip costs roughly 5.5% (3% tax plus the ~2.5% distribution fee). Independent coverage is thin too — Knots does not appear in CoinGecko's search as of this writing — so most of what a newcomer can verify comes from the project's own pages and the pool itself.

Knots is a memecoin with an unusually legible mechanism, a live market, and a reward promise that is still a promise. The next distribution decides whether the flip is a design or a decoration.
